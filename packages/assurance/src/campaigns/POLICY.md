# Campaign Policy

A campaign is a composition mechanism, not a release ceremony.

Every campaign should complete the sentence:

> This campaign proves __________________.

If that statement requires several unrelated claims, split the campaign.

## Required cases

Only checks necessary to establish the campaign's stated claim should be
required.

## Observational cases

Metrics, diagnostics, experimental observations, and future-scope checks remain
visible without affecting qualification.

## Prohibited patterns

Do not require a check merely because:

- every historical campaign measured it;
- a previous milestone required it;
- it is useful telemetry;
- another subsystem happens to expose it;
- it makes the report look more complete.

## No generic phase checklist

There is no global requirement that every campaign observe:

- queue wait
- browser startup
- perception
- execution
- evidence finalization
- artifact retrieval
- compilation
- certification

A specific campaign may explicitly verify one of those properties when its
claim genuinely requires it.
