---
name: scry-review-results
description: Explain an existing Scry Mission or report, locate its evidence and clarify unresolved application findings. Use for result review, not permission to rerun tests or change the application.
---

Use the connected Scry tools to locate the requested Mission/report. If several
match and the customer's intent is ambiguous, offer understandable titles rather
than asking them to supply internal identifiers.

Read the report's recorded checks and limitations before drawing a conclusion.
Explain consequential unexpected behavior first, then verified outcomes and gaps.
A completed Mission or Run describes testing progress, not an application verdict.
An excluded or blocked check did not pass. Preserve the reason a result could not
be confirmed or tested; say when the report does not establish that reason. For
each unresolved check give its practical next action and owner, or say no
supported next step is known. Preserve agreed exclusions without prescribing
work to undo them. Separate failed interaction methods from the final verified
application outcome; keep requested, planned, in-flight and observed-complete
facts distinct in every field.

Locate the recordings/screenshots attached to the relevant finding using returned
references. Distinguish having a recording available from actually viewing it.
Do not claim a video was reviewed merely because its metadata loaded. Masking is
best effort and does not prove that all sensitive information was hidden. A
warning is not proof of a leak and its absence is not privacy certification.
Explain affected evidence intervals and review relevant evidence before sharing;
keep unavailable or incomplete evidence explicit. Share only returned authenticated
report/evidence links, never invented public media links.

Keep execution diagnostics separate from application findings. Suggest the
smallest useful follow-up when requested, but do not rerun journeys, modify data
or create external issues just because the customer asked to review a report.

Final reports are immutable. Preserve historical findings and source evidence;
a newer timestamp alone does not establish that another Mission corrects this
one. Explain what relevant retesting changed only when an explicit relationship
or recorded evidence supports it. A complete recording does not establish full
requested-scenario coverage. Do not claim automatic background follow-up.
